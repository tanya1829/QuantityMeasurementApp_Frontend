Measurement Reference

Quick reference guide for all standard measurement units.

W

Weight Units

kg
Kilogram
SI base unit of mass. 1 kg = 1000 g
g
Gram
1 g = 0.001 kg = 1000 mg
mg
Milligram
1 mg = 0.001 g = 0.000001 kg
µg
Microgram
1 µg = 0.001 mg = 1×10⁻⁶ g
lb
Pound
1 lb = 453.592 g = 0.453 kg
oz
Ounce
1 oz = 28.3495 g = 0.0625 lb
t
Metric Ton
1 t = 1000 kg = 1,000,000 g
N
Newton
1 N ≈ 101.97 g (force on Earth)
kN
Kilonewton
1 kN = 1000 N ≈ 101.97 kg
st
Stone
1 st = 14 lb = 6.35 kg
V

Volume Units

L
Litre
1 L = 1000 mL = 0.001 m³
mL
Millilitre
1 mL = 0.001 L = 1 cm³
µL
Microlitre
1 µL = 0.001 mL = 0.000001 L
m³
Cubic Meter
1 m³ = 1000 L = 1,000,000 mL
gal
Gallon (US)
1 gal = 3.785 L = 3785.41 mL
fl oz
Fluid Ounce
1 fl oz = 29.5735 mL
cup
Cup (US)
1 cup = 236.588 mL = 8 fl oz
L

Length Units

m
Metre
SI base unit of length. 1 m = 100 cm
cm
Centimetre
1 cm = 0.01 m = 10 mm
mm
Millimetre
1 mm = 0.1 cm = 0.001 m
km
Kilometre
1 km = 1000 m = 100,000 cm
in
Inch
1 in = 2.54 cm = 25.4 mm
ft
Foot
1 ft = 12 in = 30.48 cm
mi
Mile
1 mi = 1.609 km = 5280 ft
T

Temperature Units

°C
Celsius
Water freezes at 0°C, boils at 100°C
°F
Fahrenheit
°F = (°C × 9/5) + 32
K
Kelvin
K = °C + 273.15. Absolute zero = 0 K
°R
Rankine
°R = (°C + 273.15) × 9/5
